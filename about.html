About Da Kine Grindz

Da Kine Grindz by Imi is inspired by my passion for cooking and sharing food with others. Growing up in Hawaiʻi, food has always been a way to bring people together. My dream is to open a restaurant that serves comforting, local-style grindz made with aloha.

This website is part of my journey toward that dream. As I learn more about web development and business, Da Kine Grindz will continue to grow and evolve.

Our Values

  • Fresh, local ingredients
  • Cooking with aloha
  • Sharing food that brings people together
  • Supporting local farms and businesses
